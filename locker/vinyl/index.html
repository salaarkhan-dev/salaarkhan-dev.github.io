About the Artist

No image
on file

A five-piece group of white New Orleans musicians who reached a New York studio in February 1917, ahead of the Black players whose music they had learned in the same city. Their record sold in the hundreds of thousands and fixed jazz in the popular imagination as something it largely was not.

Why This Record Is Here

Being first to ship is not the same as having invented the thing. I keep this one because it is the cleanest reminder that history records whoever got to the studio, not whoever did the work — and that the two are worth telling apart.
